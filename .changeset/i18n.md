The interface of the components is translated in English and French. Its language is the `ui-lang` attribute of the component or of an ancestor, else the page language (`<html lang>`), else English; the `lang` attribute of `ed-speech` stays the language of the answer. `registerMessages(locale, messages)`, exported by the package, changes messages or adds a language. In English, the submit button now reads "Check" instead of "Correction", and numbers shown by `ed-num` follow the interface language (`3,14` in French).
