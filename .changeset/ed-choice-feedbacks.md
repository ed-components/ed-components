`ed-choice` shows feedbacks once the learner answered: a blockquote under an answer is the feedback of this answer, shown when it is chosen, as correct or incorrect; a blockquote right after the list is the feedback of the question, such as the solution. Feedbacks are displayed by a new `ed-feedback` element, hidden until revealed and announced to screen readers. In `ed-pb`, the question feedback stays hidden with its question.
