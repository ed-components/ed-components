`ed-num` and `ed-speech` show feedbacks once the learner answered: the score with the correct answer, as correct or incorrect, then the feedback of the question, a blockquote after the answer (`ed-num`) or at the end of the question (`ed-speech`). In `ed-pb`, a blockquote right after a `:num:` list is the feedback of its `ed-num`.
